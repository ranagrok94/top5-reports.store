About Us

Last updated: July 2026

Top 3 Joint Supplements is an independent consumer information site. We publish plain-language comparisons of joint health supplements sold directly to consumers in the United States, so that readers can see how competing products differ before they spend money.

Why this site exists

The joint supplement category is crowded, and most of the material written about it falls into one of two camps: marketing copy paid for by a brand, or blanket dismissal that treats every product as a scam. Neither is much help to someone deciding what to buy. We try to occupy the space in between — describing what a formula is designed to do, what the published evidence supports, and where it stops.

How we review

Every product we cover is scored against the same five criteria, published openly on the page where the comparison appears:

  • Active ingredients — whether they are clinically studied, and whether the dose is disclosed on the label rather than hidden inside a proprietary blend.
  • Mechanism — what part of the problem the formula actually acts on.
  • Effectiveness — reported outcomes past the one-month mark, rather than first-week enthusiasm.
  • Value for money — real cost per serving measured against what the formula covers.
  • Guarantees — the length of the return window and how difficult it is to actually use.

Our reviews are based on manufacturer labels, publicly available scientific literature, and publicly posted customer feedback. We are not a laboratory. We do not run clinical trials, and we do not perform independent chemical assays on finished products. Where we cite research, we cite the source so you can read it yourself.

How we are funded

This site earns money through affiliate commissions. When a reader buys a product after clicking one of our links, the retailer may pay us a commission. This costs the reader nothing extra.

You should read our rankings with that in mind. We think a funding model is only acceptable if it is disclosed plainly, which is why it appears here, on every review page, and in our Disclaimer.
