Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it to answer you and keep a record of the exchange.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, ours records technical data when you visit: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, time spent, and how you scroll and click. We use this to understand which parts of a page people read and where they lose interest.

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive personal information through this site. Please do not send us health details, medical history, or payment card numbers.

Analytics and session recording

We use third-party analytics services, including Microsoft Clarity, which records anonymised session activity such as mouse movement, scrolling, and clicks, and aggregates it into heatmaps. This helps us see which explanations work and which do not. These services set their own cookies and process data under their own privacy terms.

Affiliate links and tracking

This site contains affiliate links. When you click one, the destination retailer or its affiliate network may set a cookie or append tracking parameters to the URL in order to attribute a resulting purchase to us and pay a commission.

That tracking is performed by the retailer and its network, not by us, and is governed by their privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. We may be told that a sale occurred, but not who made it.

How we use information

  • To operate the site and keep it working correctly and securely.
  • To understand how our content is read, and improve it.
  • To measure whether our advertising is reaching the right audience.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with service providers who operate parts of this site on our behalf — hosting, content delivery, analytics, and advertising measurement — and only as needed for those functions. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights.

Note that some privacy laws, including in California, define "sale" and "sharing" broadly enough to cover certain advertising and analytics cookies. If you wish to opt out of that, see the section below.

Cookies and your choices

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers offer a "do not track" signal. Blocking analytics cookies will not stop you from reading this site. Advertising identifiers can usually also be reset in your device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how it is processed, request a copy in portable form, or opt out of its sale or sharing. Exercising these rights is free and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

To make a request, email contact@top5-reports.store. We may need to ask you for information to verify who you are before we act.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires. Analytics data is retained in aggregated or anonymised form.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, where data protection law may differ from that of your jurisdiction.
